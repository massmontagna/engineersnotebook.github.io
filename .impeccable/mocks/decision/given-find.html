Beam deflection

Given
PPoint load at mid-span2.0 kN
LSpan, simply supported1.50 m
ESteel200 GPa
b×hRectangular section40 × 80 mm
Find

Maximum deflection δ at mid-span.

Solution
I = bh³/12 = 1.71 × 10⁶ mm⁴
δ = PL³ / 48EI
= 2000 × 1.5³ / (48 × 200×10⁹ × 1.71×10⁻⁶)
Answer
δ = 0.412 mm
